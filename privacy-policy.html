Introduction

This privacy policy explains how Match Cards: Memory Game ("we", "our", "the app") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

The app itself does not collect any personal information directly. However, the app uses third-party services that may collect information:

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use the following data:

  • Advertising ID (Android)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • IP address (for approximate location-based ads)
  • App usage data and ad interaction data

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play Billing

The app offers optional in-app purchases for virtual coins. These purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Data Storage

All game progress (levels, scores, coins, power-ups) is stored locally on your device. We do not store any game data on external servers.

Children's Privacy

The app is rated for ages 3+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The advertisements shown are configured to comply with applicable regulations.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any personal information with third parties, except as described above through Google AdMob for advertising purposes.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
